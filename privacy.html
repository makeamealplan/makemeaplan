Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 2026

MakeMeAPlan ("we", "us", "our") operates the MakeMeAPlan mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Anonymous device identifier. When you first open the App, we generate a random anonymous ID and store it on your device. We use it to associate the plans you create with your device. It is not linked to your name, email, or phone number.
  • Plan content. The goal you type, the answers you pick in the wizard, and the AI-generated plan are stored on our servers so you can access your plans across sessions.
  • Purchase status. If you unlock a plan or subscribe, we store the fact that you unlocked it. Card details are handled by Apple, Google, or our payment processor — we never see or store them.
  • Diagnostic information. Basic crash and error logs to keep the App working.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate your personalized plan using AI.
  • To let you view, edit, and download your plans.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. AI-generated content

Plans are generated by third-party AI models (OpenAI). Your goal and answers are sent to the AI provider to produce the plan. We do not send your device identifier to the AI provider.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share limited data only with:

  • Our cloud hosting provider (to run the App).
  • The AI provider (to generate your plan).
  • The app store you purchased through (Apple or Google) for payment processing.

5. Data retention

Your plans stay in our system until you delete them. You can delete a plan any time from inside the App. To delete your entire account and all plans, email makemeaplan.help@gmail.com.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it by emailing makemeaplan.help@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be announced in the App.

10. Contact

Questions? Email makemeaplan.help@gmail.com.
